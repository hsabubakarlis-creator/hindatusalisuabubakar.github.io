Dr. Hindatu Salisu Abubakar, Librarian of Katsina State Institute of Technology and Management, Katsina State. 
Dr. Hindatu Salisu Abubakar is a distinguished academic and an administrator. She earned her Bachelor Degree, Master Degree and a PhD in Library and Information Science all from Bayero University, Kano. 

Dr. Hindatu  began her academic career at Bayero University in 2012, ascending to the rank of Senior Lecturer in 2023. In December 2024, She became the Ag. Rector and also the Director Academics of the Institute coupled with her position as a librarian.

My Area of Research interest are Information Science, children’s Library Services, Readership promotions, library advocacy and community services.
